Privacy Policy

Last updated: 11 August 2026

FloorScore ("the app") is installed by a Shopify merchant ("you", "the merchant") to track staff sales attribution and calculate commission. This policy describes what data the app collects, why, and how it's handled.

What we collect

From the Shopify store that installs the app, we collect and store:

  • Your shop's domain, currency, and timezone.
  • Staff identifiers from your POS sales (the Shopify staff ID attached to each sale), and any name you choose to give that person inside the app - never staff PINs, passwords, or login credentials. Shopify only shares staff names with apps on Plus and Advanced plans, so on other plans the name shown is the one you set.
  • POS sale and refund records: amount, currency, timestamp, location, and which staff member the sale is attributed to.
  • Your chosen plan and subscription/billing status (via Shopify's Billing API).

We do not collect or store your customers' personal information (name, email, address, or any other end-customer PII) as part of this app's functionality - only the transaction-level data listed above.

Why we collect it

Solely to power the app's features: the live staff leaderboard, commission calculation, and payroll summaries/exports you generate.

Who can see it

Staff sales data is visible within your own store's admin (to you) and on the public leaderboard link the app generates - that link is a long, random, unguessable token; anyone who has the exact link can view it, so treat it like you would a shared screen password, and only display it where your staff and customers can see it.

How long we keep it

Sales history is kept for 24 months, then deleted automatically. That period covers year-over-year comparisons and gives you a margin for payroll or tax questions, without keeping records indefinitely.

When you uninstall the app, your shop and staff data is deleted within 30 days, in line with Shopify's data protection requirements for apps - you don't have to wait for the 24-month period to elapse.

Third parties

We use Shopify's own Billing API for all charges - no separate payment processor ever sees your payment details.

The services that process data on our behalf:

  • Supabase - hosts the database holding the data described above. Data is stored on AWS in the Asia Pacific (Tokyo) region.
  • Google Cloud Run (Google LLC) - runs the application itself, in the Asia Pacific (Tokyo) region.
  • Sentry (error monitoring) and PostHog (product analytics) - optional, and only receive diagnostic and usage information such as "leaderboard opened", never your customers' personal information.

We'll update this list before adding any new service that processes your data.

Your rights

You can request a copy of, or deletion of, the data described above at any time by contacting us (see below), or by using Shopify's own data request/erasure tools.
