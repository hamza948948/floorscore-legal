The service

What we process, and why

Only what's needed to attribute POS sales to staff and calculate commission: the staff member, retail location, order identifier, sale amount, currency, item count and timestamps. We request the read_orders and read_locations scopes and nothing else.

We do not store customer names, emails, phone numbers or addresses. Order webhooks contain customer details; the app discards them at the point of ingestion rather than saving and filtering them later. There is no customer record in our database.
